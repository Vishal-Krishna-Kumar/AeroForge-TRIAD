# Experiment protocol v1 (pre-registered before any surrogate is trained) — AeroForge-TRIAD

- **Status:** DRAFT v1, 2026-10-12. Written before Dataset V1 exists and before any surrogate is trained, so
  the analysis choices below cannot be tuned on results. Any later change is logged in §9 with its reason
  and date, and both versions are reported.
- **Scope:** hypotheses H1–H4 of `research/hypotheses/primary_research_question.md`.

## 1. Data

- Dataset V1 (ADR-003): Re = 1000, θ = (m, p, t, α), P-coarse mesh, splits train 512 / cal 128 / test 128
  (independent scrambled Sobol sequences). Final box = the steady envelope from the regime map.
- cal and test cases carry discrete-adjoint gradients of CL and CD (verified against FD before use).
- Optimiser-trajectory data (H2/H4) is generated by independent optimiser runs (§5) and split **by run**.

## 2. Surrogates (all trained on the train split only)

| ID | Model | Training signal | Ensemble |
|---|---|---|---|
| S1 | FNO on the SDF grid (models.surrogates.FNOSurrogate) | fields + CL/CD | 5 seeds |
| S2 | MLP θ → (CL, CD) | CL/CD | 5 seeds |
| S3 | FNO, derivative-informed (Sobolev term) | fields + CL/CD + adjoint gradients on a 128-case train subset | 5 seeds |

Hyper-parameters are chosen on a validation fold carved from train (last 64 Sobol points), never on cal/test.

## 3. H1 — gradient gap (test split, i.i.d.)

Metrics per test point: relative coefficient error |ĉ − c|/|c| for CL, CD, L/D; gradient cosine similarity
cos(∇̂J, ∇J) in the box-normalised metric M = diag(span²); relative gradient error ‖∇̂J − ∇J‖_M / ‖∇J‖_M.
H1 is **supported** if, for S1, the median relative gradient error exceeds 3× the median relative L/D
error **and** cos < 0.98 on more than 5 % of test points. **Falsified** if cos ≥ 0.98 on ≥ 95 % of test points.

## 4. H2 — calibration (CDGC)

Score s = ‖∇J − ∇̂J‖_M / σ̂, σ̂ = ensemble standard deviation of ∇̂J in the M-norm (floored at 1e-6 of the
median). Split conformal at α ∈ {0.1, 0.2}. Coverage on test (i.i.d.) and on held-out optimiser trajectories
(§5). Repeated over 5 ensemble seeds/cal resamplings. **Falsified** if empirical coverage < 1 − α − 0.05
on trajectories, averaged over seeds.

## 5. H3 — decision value (optimiser)

- Objective: maximise L/D at Re 1000 over the box. To avoid optima pinned at box corners, the primary task is
  **max L/D subject to t ≥ 0.10 and CL ≥ 0.25** (quadratic penalty, fixed weight 100, fixed before data).
  The unconstrained L/D problem is reported as a secondary task.
- Optimiser: projected normalised-gradient ascent, step 0.05 of the box span, 30 steps, in-loop CFD budget
  B ∈ {5, 10}. Starts: 10 Sobol points (seed 7) per method, identical across methods.
- Gates: never, always (budget-limited), CDGC (α = 0.1), output-UQ (threshold = 90th percentile of ensemble
  std of J on cal), distance (threshold = 90th percentile of nearest-neighbour distance cal→train).
- Every 5th iterate and the final iterate of every run are CFD-verified post hoc (primal) for the failed-step
  metric (see change log v1.1); final designs get primal + adjoint.
- Primary metric: CFD-verified objective of the final design vs in-loop CFD calls. Secondary: fraction of
  CFD-verified failed steps (verified objective decreases).
- Test: paired Wilcoxon signed-rank across the 10 starts, CDGC vs output-UQ (H3's comparator), two-sided,
  α = 0.05, Holm correction across the two budgets. **Falsified** if no significant improvement over output-UQ.

## 6. H4 — shift

Coverage of i.i.d.-calibrated certificates on trajectory points vs trajectory-calibrated (run-level split:
calibration runs and test runs from disjoint starting points). Report both, with run-level bootstrap CIs.

## 7. Compute and reproducibility

All CFD on one platform (Colab runtime image recorded in env/). Seeds fixed and listed. Every number in the
paper is regenerated by a script from stored artefacts.

## 8. What would make us stop

- Adjoint vs FD disagreement > 2 % on the verification case → no gradient-based claims until resolved.
- Dataset convergence failures > 5 % of cases → revisit the envelope before training.

## 9. Change log
